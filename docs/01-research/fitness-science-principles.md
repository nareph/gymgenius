# 1. Objective

This document defines the scientific foundations behind GymGenius v3.

Every decision made by the system must be aligned with validated principles from:

* Exercise physiology
* Strength and hypertrophy research
* Nutrition science
* Recovery science
* Behavioral adaptation principles

The goal is to ensure GymGenius is not opinion-based, but **evidence-based**.

---

# 2. Core Scientific Pillars

GymGenius is built on five foundational pillars.

---

## 2.1 Progressive Overload

> The body adapts only to increasing stress.

### Definition

Progressive overload is the gradual increase of:

* weight
* reps
* sets
* intensity
* density (less rest time)

### Application in GymGenius

The Workout Engine must ensure:

* every cycle increases at least one variable
* stagnation triggers adaptation changes
* overload is controlled, not maximal

### Rule

> No progression = no improvement.

---

## 2.2 Energy Balance

> Body weight changes depend on calorie balance.

### Principles

* Caloric surplus → weight gain
* Caloric deficit → weight loss
* Maintenance → stability

### GymGenius Application

The Nutrition Engine estimates:

* TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)
* Adjusts based on trend data
* Corrects based on real-world weight evolution

### Key Insight

> Real weight trends override theoretical calculations.

---

## 2.3 Protein Synthesis & Muscle Growth

> Muscle growth requires sufficient protein and resistance training.

### Scientific Baseline

* 1.6g/kg → minimum effective dose
* 2.0–2.2g/kg → optimal hypertrophy range

### GymGenius Application

The system:

* calculates protein targets per user
* distributes protein across meals
* adjusts based on training intensity

---

## 2.4 Recovery as a Limiting Factor

> Adaptation occurs during recovery, not training.

### Key Variables

* sleep quality
* sleep duration
* fatigue accumulation
* training load
* stress levels

### GymGenius Application

The Recovery Engine:

* calculates recovery score (0–100)
* reduces training intensity when needed
* triggers rest days dynamically

### Rule

> Poor recovery = reduced adaptation potential.

---

## 2.5 Individual Variability

> No two bodies respond identically.

### Implications

GymGenius must adapt to:

* genetic differences (indirectly observed)
* lifestyle differences
* adherence differences
* recovery differences

### System Design

Instead of fixed rules:

* GymGenius uses adaptive models
* behavior-driven adjustments
* long-term trend learning

---

# 3. Training Science Principles

---

## 3.1 Hypertrophy Mechanisms

Muscle growth is driven by:

* mechanical tension (primary driver)
* metabolic stress
* muscle damage (secondary role)

### GymGenius Implementation

Workout Engine prioritizes:

* controlled eccentric movements
* compound lifts
* progressive load increase

---

## 3.2 Optimal Volume

### Research Range

* 10–20 sets per muscle group per week (intermediate range)

### GymGenius Rules

* Beginners → lower volume
* Intermediate → moderate volume
* Advanced → higher volume

### Adaptive Rule

> Volume is adjusted based on recovery capacity, not fixed templates.

---

## 3.3 Training Frequency

### Principle

Training a muscle 2x per week is generally optimal for hypertrophy.

### GymGenius Implementation

* Split selection ensures frequency balance
* Full-body / Upper-Lower / PPL selected dynamically

---

## 3.4 Intensity Zones

| Goal        | Reps Range |
| ----------- | ---------- |
| Strength    | 3–6        |
| Hypertrophy | 6–12       |
| Endurance   | 12–20      |

### Application

GymGenius assigns rep ranges based on:

* goal
* fatigue state
* exercise type

---

## 3.5 Failure Training

> Training to failure is a tool, not a default.

### Rule

* Used selectively
* Avoided in high fatigue states
* Controlled for compound lifts

---

# 4. Nutrition Science Principles

---

## 4.1 Caloric Control

Weight change is driven by:

* energy intake
* energy expenditure

### System Rule

GymGenius adjusts calories based on:

* weekly weight trend
* adherence level
* training load

---

## 4.2 Macronutrient Priorities

### Priority Order

1. Protein (muscle preservation/growth)
2. Fat (hormonal function)
3. Carbohydrates (performance energy)

---

## 4.3 Meal Timing

> Total intake matters more than timing, but timing improves performance.

### Guidelines

* pre-workout carbs improve performance
* post-workout protein supports recovery
* meal distribution improves adherence

---

## 4.4 Food Quality vs Flexibility

GymGenius follows a hybrid approach:

* 80% whole foods
* 20% flexible intake

---

# 5. Recovery Science Principles

---

## 5.1 Sleep is the Primary Recovery Driver

### Effects of poor sleep:

* reduced protein synthesis
* increased fatigue
* reduced performance
* higher injury risk

---

## 5.2 System Response

If sleep is low:

* reduce training intensity
* prioritize recovery nutrition
* avoid failure training

---

## 5.3 Fatigue Accumulation

Fatigue is:

* cumulative
* systemic
* non-linear

GymGenius tracks:

* short-term fatigue
* long-term fatigue

---

## 5.4 Overtraining Prevention

True overtraining is rare, but overreaching is common.

GymGenius prevents:

* excessive volume spikes
* insufficient rest cycles
* progressive fatigue buildup
