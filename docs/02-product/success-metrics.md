# GymGenius v3

## Success Metrics

Version: 3.0

---

# 1. Overview

This document defines how GymGenius measures product success.

Unlike traditional fitness apps, success is not measured by usage alone, but by **user transformation over time**.

---

# 2. Core Success Philosophy

GymGenius is successful if:

> Users become healthier, more consistent, and more autonomous in their fitness journey.

Not just if they open the app.

---

# 3. Primary Success Metrics

---

## 3.1 User Retention (Long-Term)

### Definition

Ability to retain users over weeks and months.

### Key Indicators

* 7-day retention
* 30-day retention
* 90-day retention

### Interpretation

High retention = system provides continuous value.

Low retention = recommendations are not adaptive enough.

---

## 3.2 Training Consistency Rate

### Definition

Percentage of planned workouts completed.

### Formula

```
consistency = completed_workouts / planned_workouts
```

### Target

* Beginner: 60–75%
* Intermediate: 70–85%
* Advanced: 80–95%

---

## 3.3 Physical Progress Index

Measures real physical improvement.

### Components

* Weight trend (goal-dependent)
* Strength progression
* Body recomposition signals

### Interpretation

Positive trend = system is effective.

Flat trend = plateau detection required.

---

## 3.4 Recovery Efficiency Score

Measures how well the system manages fatigue.

### Inputs

* sleep quality
* fatigue levels
* injury prevention signals

### Target

* Stable or improving recovery trends over time

---

## 3.5 Goal Achievement Rate

### Definition

How often users reach their defined goals.

### Examples

* muscle gain goal reached
* fat loss target achieved
* strength milestones completed

---

# 4. Behavioral Metrics

---

## 4.1 Decision Fatigue Reduction

### Goal

Minimize user decision-making effort.

### Indicators

* time spent deciding workouts
* number of manual adjustments
* reliance on AI suggestions

---

## 4.2 Daily Engagement Simplicity

GymGenius should require:

* < 2 minutes input per day

Measured by:

* average session setup time
* interaction complexity

---

## 4.3 Habit Formation Index

Measures how automated user behavior becomes.

### Signals

* workouts performed at consistent times
* reduced skipped days
* stable routine adherence

---

# 5. System Performance Metrics

---

## 5.1 Response Time

### Target

* < 200ms local operations
* instant workout display
* no blocking UI

---

## 5.2 Offline Reliability

### Definition

System must function fully without internet.

### Metrics

* 100% core functionality offline
* zero dependency failures

---

## 5.3 Data Integrity

### Requirements

* no lost workout logs
* no corrupted routines
* consistent historical tracking

---

# 6. AI Quality Metrics

---

## 6.1 Recommendation Accuracy

Measures how relevant AI suggestions are.

### Evaluation Signals

* user acceptance rate of recommendations
* manual overrides frequency
* progress alignment with suggestions

---

## 6.2 Explainability Score

Every recommendation must be:

* understandable
* data-backed
* traceable

### Target

* 100% recommendations explained

---

## 6.3 Adaptation Speed

Measures how quickly system reacts to:

* weight changes
* missed workouts
* recovery shifts

---

# 7. Health Outcome Metrics

---

## 7.1 Strength Gains

* progressive overload improvements
* 1RM increases (future feature)

---

## 7.2 Body Composition Change

* fat loss rate
* muscle gain rate
* recomposition detection

---

## 7.3 Energy & Recovery Trends

* improved sleep quality
* reduced fatigue over time
* stable training readiness

---

# 8. Product Quality Metrics

---

## 8.1 User Satisfaction Index

Derived from:

* retention
* engagement
* feedback signals

---

## 8.2 Friction Score

Measures difficulty of using the app.

Lower is better.

Signals:

* number of inputs required
* navigation complexity
* time to start workout

---

## 8.3 System Trust Level

How much users rely on GymGenius decisions.

Signals:

* acceptance rate of recommendations
* reduction in manual overrides

---

# 9. North Star Metric

The single most important metric:

> **Weekly Health Improvement Score (WHIS)**

### Definition

Composite score combining:

* training consistency
* physical progress
* recovery stability
* nutrition adherence

---

# 10. Success Definition

GymGenius is successful if:

* users improve physically over time
* users stay consistent without burnout
* users trust system recommendations
* users reduce decision fatigue
* users maintain long-term engagement

---

# 11. Failure Signals

GymGenius is failing if:

* users abandon within 2–3 weeks
* high inconsistency rates
* no measurable progress
* excessive manual overrides
* poor recovery trends

---

# 12. Final Principle

> A fitness system is only successful if it improves human outcomes, not app metrics.
